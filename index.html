PUB Opener Privacy Policy

PUB Opener, by ByteAcre · Effective 20 November 2026 (version 1.0)

PUB Opener is a Windows app that opens Microsoft Publisher (.pub) files so you can view and print them and save them as PDF or PNG. In short: your files never leave your PC. PUB Opener converts them on your PC, sends nothing anywhere and does not collect or share any of your data.

Your files

  • PUB Opener only reads the .pub files you open (with the Open button, by dropping them on the window, by double-clicking them, or in the batch converter). It never changes them.
  • To show a file, the app converts it into pages in a temporary folder inside the app's own data folder on your PC. These pages are deleted when you open another file or close the window (and leftovers after one day at the latest).
  • PDF and PNG files are saved only where you choose.
  • Nothing from your files is uploaded, sent, analysed or shared.

Settings stored on your PC

The app saves its settings in a small file in its own folder on your PC: the theme, view options, export options, the batch converter's folders, the window position, and the list of the last 10 files you opened (their names and folders, so you can open them again). There is also a small troubleshooting log (when the app started, how many pages a file had, how long it took, and errors; it contains no file contents). Nothing of this leaves your PC, and Windows removes it all when you uninstall the app.

Internet use

  • PUB Opener itself does not connect to the internet. The pages it shows are local files; the viewer is locked so that a file can't load anything from the internet.
  • Windows and the Microsoft Store check your license (whether you have bought the app or are in the free trial). That check is done by Microsoft, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • The pages are shown with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, a part of Windows. Microsoft keeps it up to date through its own updater, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. PUB Opener turns off WebView2's optional online features that it doesn't need (such as SmartScreen look-ups and background networking).

What the app does not do

  • It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not collect, sell or share personal information.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. ByteAcre never sees your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
